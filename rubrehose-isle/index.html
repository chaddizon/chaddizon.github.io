Wash ashore

Land on Rubrehose Isle with Big Bad Wolf, Lucy, Big Bad Coyote, Lucette and Tuggy the tugboat. Break driftwood, gather shells and forage, and push your crew deeper while the storm rolls in, even while you're away.

Weather the storm

Every Tempest you clear makes the storm harder and the rewards richer. Watch the sky for Sunbreaks, the Eye and the rare Golden Eye.

Raise a flock

Light the Reef Light and the puffins move in. Bank their dives and fire them yourself, tie kerchiefs on them and feed them fish.

Down to the Deep Reef

Fight the bosses of the deep, mine Abyssite and raise carved figureheads that power every voyage.

Craft and set sail

Refine shells into pearls and forage into dyes, take on Tuggy's odd jobs, then set sail for a stronger voyage.
