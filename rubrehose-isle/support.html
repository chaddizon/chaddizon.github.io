Support

Stuck, found a bug, or a purchase didn't arrive? We're here to help.

Contact us

Email rubrehose@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

To help us fix things quickly, please include:

  • your phone model and its iOS or Android version;
  • the game's version (in your phone's settings, under apps);
  • what happened and what you were doing, with a screenshot if you can.

Common questions

Does the game keep going while I'm away?

Yes. Your crew keeps working while the game is closed, up to the offline limit, and you collect it all when you come back.

I bought something and didn't get it.

Close and reopen the game first. Then open the in-game store and tap Restore Purchases. If it still hasn't arrived, email us with your receipt (Apple sends one by email; on Google Play it's under Payments & subscriptions › Budget & history).

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds go through them: on iPhone at reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Budget & history, or at Google Play's refund page.

I got a new phone. Can I keep my progress?

Your save lives on your phone. Move it with your phone's own transfer: iCloud Backup or Quick Start on iPhone, Google One backup or the setup transfer on Android. Purchases that unlock something permanent come back with Restore Purchases.

Can I play on iPhone and Android with the same save?

Not yet. Each phone keeps its own save.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the game. Your save is stored only on your device, and we don't keep any account or data about you on a server, so uninstalling removes everything.

What do I need to play?

An iPhone, or an Android phone running Android 8.0 or later. The game plays offline; purchases need a connection.
