Privacy Policy

Rubrehose Isle · by Chadrick Dizon · Effective November 3, 2026

The short version

Rubrehose Isle has no accounts and no ads. It doesn't ask for your name, email, contacts or location, and it doesn't sell or share your personal information. Your progress is saved on your own phone.

Who we are

Rubrehose Isle ("the game") is made by Chadrick Dizon, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy covers the game on iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play) and this website.

Information the game uses

Your save

Your progress and settings are stored on your device only. We never receive them.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your name, card or payment details. To confirm a purchase, the game uses Unity's in-app purchasing service, which may process the store's purchase receipt and basic technical information (device type, operating system and app version). See Unity's privacy policy, Apple's and Google's.

Time check

To keep offline rewards fair, the game may read the current date and time from a public web server when it starts. Like any web request, this exposes your device's IP address to that server. The game sends nothing else and stores nothing from it.

Crash reports

If you've allowed your phone to share diagnostics with app developers, Apple or Google may send us anonymous crash reports. They help us fix bugs and don't identify you.

What we don't collect

No analytics, advertising ID, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or share personal information. The only services involved are the ones above (Apple, Google and Unity), each for the purpose described.

Keeping and deleting data

We don't keep personal data on our own servers. Your save stays on your device until you uninstall the game, which deletes it. If you email us, we keep the conversation only as long as needed to help you; ask us any time to delete it.

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

The game doesn't hold personal information. Purchases use Apple's and Google's own secure systems.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what we hold about you (normally nothing) and ask us to delete it. Email us and we'll answer within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Big changes will also be noted in the game's update notes.
